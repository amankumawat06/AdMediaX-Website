The Problem

This jewellery shop owner had beautiful products - rings, necklaces, earrings, and sets that genuinely deserved attention. They had tried running ads on Facebook and Instagram multiple times, spending their budget month after month. But the ROI was poor. Very few clicks, almost no new customers, and barely any engagement on posts.

They had the products. They had the budget. But what they didn't have was the one thing that makes jewellery sell online - visuals that make people stop and stare. Their ads were using dull, flat, poorly-lit photos that made even the most gorgeous pieces look ordinary. On a social feed full of professionally shot content, their ads were completely invisible.

Our Solution

  • Before running a single rupee on ads, we fixed the creatives. We planned and produced a full social media shoot - product close-ups, jewellery on model, lifestyle settings, and aspirational imagery designed specifically for Instagram and Facebook.
  • We created multiple short-form video formats - slow-motion detail shots, styling reels, and festive collection showcases.
  • We completely restructured the Meta ad campaigns - new audiences, new creatives, new ad formats matched to the right placements (Stories, Reels, Feed).
  • We built custom audiences based on jewellery interest, gifting occasions, income segments, and lookalike audiences from existing customers.
  • We continuously A/B tested different videos and images, paused underperformers, and scaled the creatives that were generating the most engagement and purchases.

Results

The moment the new videos went live in the ads, the difference was immediate. People were stopping to watch. Saves and shares went up. DMs started coming in. And most importantly - sales increased significantly within the first month itself.

The shop owner, who had been frustrated with ads for months, now had a campaign that was actually working. Customers who came in-store were saying they found the shop on Instagram. New buyers who had never heard of the brand before were now walking in - because the videos had made the jewellery look exactly as beautiful as it truly was.
