The Problem

Our client was a real estate developer with multiple well-located residential projects - flats, plots, and independent homes at competitive prices. Despite having a genuinely good product, their phone barely rang. They were running ads on Facebook and Instagram, spending money every month, and seeing almost nothing in return.

The real problem was simple: their visuals were terrible. Blurry smartphone photos. No property walkthrough videos. No lifestyle imagery. No drone shots. In a world where a buyer's first impression of a property happens on a screen, their ads looked completely unconvincing. People scrolled past without stopping - and the developer had no idea why their budget was disappearing with zero results.

Our Solution

  • Before touching any ad campaign, we identified the root cause: the creative assets. No amount of targeting or budget can fix a bad visual. So we started from the ground up.
  • We conducted a professional on-site production - cinematic walkthrough videos, DSLR photography, interior close-ups, and lifestyle imagery across all available units.
  • We produced scroll-stopping Instagram and Facebook reels showcasing the property's best features - views, amenities, and location benefits.
  • We built a consistent posting calendar covering property USPs, neighbourhood highlights, and builder credibility content.
  • We launched location-specific lead generation ads with custom audiences - targeting by income level, property-buying intent, and local area interest.
  • We set up retargeting to re-engage users who watched the videos or visited the landing page but hadn't submitted an inquiry yet.

Results

Within the first week of launching campaigns with the new visuals, inquiries started coming in. By month two, the transformation was undeniable. The same ad budget that was producing almost nothing was now delivering consistent, qualified leads - buyers who had already watched the property video and were ready to visit.

The developer's site visits doubled. Buyers were arriving already informed, which also shortened the sales cycle. The project that was stuck for months began closing units consistently.
